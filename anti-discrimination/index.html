Notice of Nondiscrimination

Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center complies with applicable federal civil rights laws and does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national origin, age, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or religion.

Accessibility

Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center provides:

  • Reasonable modifications to policies, practices, and procedures when necessary to accommodate individuals with disabilities
