Texas Consumer Notice

This page gives patients instructions for requesting health care records, contacting the licensing authorities that regulate this practice, and filing a consumer complaint. It is provided under Texas Health and Safety Code Section 181.105 (House Bill 4224, effective September 1, 2025).

1. How to request your health care records

Submit a written request in person during business hours, or by mail, to the location where you received care. You may also call that location or the main line at (254) 410-6888 to start a request.

Please include:

  • Your full name
  • Your date of birth
  • Your preferred contact information
  • The dates of service you are requesting
  • How you would like the records delivered

We may need to verify your identity before records are released. Reasonable copying and mailing fees allowed by Texas law may apply. We will process requests in accordance with applicable Texas law and HIPAA.

2. How to contact the licensing authorities

Anthony Medical & Chiropractic Center includes medical and chiropractic care. The licensing authority that applies depends on the type of provider involved in your care.

Texas Medical Board

Physicians and other licensees and registrants of the Texas Medical Board, including acupuncturists where applicable, are regulated by the Texas Medical Board.

Texas Medical Board
Attention: Investigations
1801 Congress Avenue, Suite 9.200
P.O. Box 2018
Austin, Texas 78768-2018

Assistance in filing a complaint: 1-800-201-9353
Website: www.tmb.texas.gov (also listed by the Board as www.tmb.state.tx.us)

Texas Board of Chiropractic Examiners

Chiropractors are regulated by the Texas Board of Chiropractic Examiners.

Texas Board of Chiropractic Examiners
1801 Congress Avenue, Suite 10.500

Phone: (512) 305-6700

3. How to file a consumer complaint

Under Texas Health and Safety Code Section 181.103, you may file a consumer complaint with the Office of the Attorney General of Texas, Consumer Protection Division.

  • Online: texasattorneygeneral.gov/consumer-protection/file-consumer-complaint
  • Phone: 1-800-621-0508
  • Mail: Office of the Attorney General, Consumer Protection Division, P.O. Box 12548, Austin, Texas 78711-2548

You may also file a complaint with the applicable licensing board using the contacts in section 2.

NOTICE CONCERNING COMPLAINTS

Complaints about physicians, as well as other licensees and registrants of the Texas Medical Board, including physician assistants, acupuncturists, surgical assistants, medical radiologic technologists, non-certified radiologic technicians, respiratory care practitioners, medical physicists, and perfusionists may be reported for investigation at the following address:

Texas Medical Board
